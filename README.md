# FlexiBill – Pay Bills Even When You’re Short

FlexiBill helps people pay essential bills on time—even when they cannot cover the full amount upfront—by letting them save toward goals, make partial payments, and invite friends or family to contribute.

## Problem

Essential bills like rent, school fees, hospital charges, and utility payments often come due when people have less cash on hand than they need. This leads to late payments, service disruption, stress, and reliance on expensive borrowing.

## Solution

FlexiBill provides structured bill goals, flexible deposits, merchant-connected partial payments, and collaborative top-ups so users can stay current without scrambling for last-minute funds.

## How It Works

1. **Create a bill goal:** Define the amount, due date, and merchant (e.g., landlord, school, clinic). FlexiBill calculates recommended daily/weekly contributions and sends reminders.
2. **Fund the goal anytime:** Deposit small amounts from mobile money, card, or bank as often as you like. Contributions update progress in real time.
3. **Send partial payments to merchants:** Once a configurable threshold is reached (e.g., 30%), pay directly from the goal balance. Merchants see incoming partials and the remaining balance.
4. **Invite others to help:** Generate a shareable link so friends or family can contribute small amounts (50, 100, 500, etc.) to the specific goal.
5. **Close out on time:** Continue funding until the goal is fully paid. Reliability history is recorded for future perks like discounts or micro top-ups.

## Key Features

- **Bill Goals:** Set targets (e.g., "Rent 15,000 by 30 Nov") with saving guidance and anytime deposits via mobile money or card.
- **Partial Merchant Payments:** Send partial payments once a minimum threshold is reached so partners see steady progress and keep services active.
- **Friends & Family Top-Up:** Share a contribution link for others to add funds directly to a specific bill goal.
- **Smart Reminders & Alerts:** Nudges to stay on track, with options to increase contributions, request help, or adjust deadlines if allowed.
- **Bill History & Reliability Score:** Track punctuality and build a score that can unlock discounts, perks, or micro top-up credit in the future.

## User Journeys

- **Parent paying school fees:** Sets a goal for next term, funds weekly via mobile money, and shares the link with a relative to cover the last 20% before deadline.
- **Tenant handling rent:** Splits rent into weekly deposits, triggers a partial payment at 50% to reassure the landlord, and completes the rest before month end.
- **Patient settling a hospital bill:** Saves daily toward the discharge balance, sends partials to keep treatment active, and invites a friend to top up.
- **Student covering exam fees:** Uses reminders to stay on track, contributes small amounts after casual jobs, and extends the due date when the institution allows it.

## Merchant Experience

- **Visibility:** Merchants see customer-level progress, expected completion dates, and partial payments received.
- **Control:** Configure minimum partial thresholds (e.g., 30%), accepted payment methods, and whether deadline extensions are allowed.
- **Reconciliation:** Receive payouts net of fees with references tied to specific goals; exportable statements reduce back-office friction.
- **Incentives:** Offer discounts or flexibility to users with high reliability scores to encourage on-time completion.

## Safeguards & Trust

- **Payment integrity:** All user contributions remain locked to the chosen bill until paid out to the merchant or refunded if the bill is cancelled.
- **Fraud prevention:** Verified merchants, unique contribution links, rate limits on link payments, and KYC on large deposits.
- **User protection:** Clear fee disclosures, reminders before auto partial payouts, and optional spending limits to avoid over-extension.
- **Data privacy:** Minimal data collection, merchant-facing data is scoped to the specific goal, and exports avoid PII wherever possible.

## Business Model

- Transaction fees from participating merchants.
- Optional premium plan for users (enhanced reminders, analytics, and priority support).
- Future partnerships with lenders for responsible micro top-up credit to trusted users.

## Rollout Roadmap

1. **MVP (Weeks 1–8):**
   - Goal setup, deposits, and reminders.
   - Partial payment rails for early merchant partners (schools/landlords/clinics).
   - Shareable contribution links with basic progress tracking.
2. **Reliability & Insights (Weeks 9–12):**
   - Reliability score, bill history, and simple analytics.
   - Merchant dashboard to view customer progress and confirmations.
3. **Growth & Partnerships (Weeks 13+):**
   - Premium user features and expanded payment methods.
   - Pilot micro top-up credit with safeguards and affordability checks.

## Success Metrics

- Goal completion rate and on-time payment rate.
- Merchant retention and share of bills receiving partial payments.
- Contribution link conversion (views → contributions) and average top-up size.
- User Net Promoter Score and repeat goal creation.
