# FlexiBill – Pay Bills Even When You’re Short

FlexiBill helps people pay essential bills on time—even when they cannot cover the full amount upfront—by letting them save toward goals, make partial payments, and invite friends or family to contribute.

## Problem

Essential bills like rent, school fees, hospital charges, and utility payments often come due when people have less cash on hand than they need. This leads to late payments, service disruption, stress, and reliance on expensive borrowing.

## Solution

FlexiBill provides structured bill goals, flexible deposits, merchant-connected partial payments, and collaborative top-ups so users can stay current without scrambling for last-minute funds.

## Key Features

- **Bill Goals:** Set targets (e.g., "Rent 15,000 by 30 Nov"), with daily or weekly saving guidance and anytime deposits via mobile money or card.
- **Partial Merchant Payments:** Send partial payments once a minimum threshold is reached (e.g., 30%) so partners see steady progress and keep services active.
- **Friends & Family Top-Up:** Generate a shareable link for others to contribute small amounts directly to a specific bill goal.
- **Smart Reminders & Alerts:** Nudges to stay on track, with options to increase contributions, request help, or adjust deadlines if allowed.
- **Bill History & Reliability Score:** Track punctuality and build a score that can unlock discounts, perks, or micro top-up credit in the future.

## Who Uses FlexiBill?

- Students managing exam or fee balances.
- Parents coordinating multiple school payments.
- Young professionals paying rent, internet, or medical bills.
- Clinics, schools, landlords, and ISPs who want predictable, transparent payments.

## Business Model

- Transaction fees from participating merchants.
- Optional premium plan for users (enhanced reminders, analytics, and priority support).
- Future partnerships with lenders for responsible micro top-up credit to trusted users.

## Rollout Roadmap

1. **MVP (Weeks 1–8):**
   - Goal setup, deposits, and reminders.
   - Partial payment rails for early merchant partners (schools/landlords/clinics).
   - Shareable contribution links with basic progress tracking.
2. **Reliability & Insights (Weeks 9–12):**
   - Reliability score, bill history, and simple analytics.
   - Merchant dashboard to view customer progress and confirmations.
3. **Growth & Partnerships (Weeks 13+):**
   - Premium user features and expanded payment methods.
   - Pilot micro top-up credit with safeguards and affordability checks.

## Success Metrics

- Goal completion rate and on-time payment rate.
- Merchant retention and share of bills receiving partial payments.
- Contribution link conversion (views → contributions) and average top-up size.
- User Net Promoter Score and repeat goal creation.
